## How we decide what to build next

We prioritize on three axes: creator pain (how often the community asks for it), platform leverage (does it unblock many other features?), and build cost. If something you want isn't on the roadmap, tell us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/helixgame) &mdash; Creator feedback directly feeds into the next sprint.
