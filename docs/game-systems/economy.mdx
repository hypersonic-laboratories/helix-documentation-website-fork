# Economy & LIX

**LIX** is the platform currency in HELIX — think of it like Robux or V-Bucks. Players purchase LIX with real money and spend it across worlds on cosmetics, game passes, power-ups, or anything else creators offer. As a creator, LIX is how you earn revenue from your experiences.

## How LIX Works

Players buy LIX through the HELIX website. Once they have a balance, they can spend it across the platform. The flow looks like this:

1. **Player buys LIX** with real currency through the HELIX website
2. **Player spends LIX** on in-world purchases, Vault packages, collectibles, or platform services
3. **Creator earns LIX** from those purchases (as "Earned LIX")
4. **Creator cashes out** by converting Earned LIX to real currency through the payout system

LIX is also used by creators for platform services like [Instant Hosting](../publishing/instant-hosting.mdx).

:::note
HELIX separates **Purchased LIX** (bought with real money, can be spent but not cashed out) from **Earned LIX** (gained through creator activity, eligible for payout). This prevents abuse and keeps the economy stable.
:::

## Setting Up Monetization

To sell things in your world, you create **packages** in the HELIX Creator Dashboard. Each package has a name, description, LIX price, and a callback that fires when a player completes the purchase.

Common package types include:

- **Cosmetics** — character skins, vehicle wraps, emotes
- **Game passes** — VIP access, special abilities, extra inventory slots
- **Consumables** — in-game currency boosts, XP multipliers, health packs
- **Subscriptions** — recurring access to premium features

## Pricing Your Packages

When setting prices, keep your audience in mind. Here are some general guidelines:

| Package Type | Suggested LIX Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Small consumable | 10-50 LIX | Low friction, high volume |
| Cosmetic item | 50-200 LIX | Players pay more for visual flair |
| Game pass | 200-1,000 LIX | One-time unlock with lasting value |
| Premium bundle | 1,000-5,000 LIX | Multiple items at a discount |

These are suggestions, not rules. Experiment and see what works for your community.

## Creator Payouts

HELIX takes a platform fee from each transaction and passes the rest to you. Payouts are processed on a regular schedule, and you can track your earnings, transaction history, and pending payouts in the Creator Dashboard.

To be eligible for payouts, you need to:

- Complete identity verification in your creator profile
- Reach the minimum payout threshold
- Have a connected payment method (bank account or PayPal)

## In-World Economy

Beyond LIX, many creators build **secondary economies** inside their worlds using the database system. You can create in-game currencies (gold, credits, tokens) that players earn through gameplay and spend at NPC shops or player-to-player trades. These economies run entirely on your server and don't involve real money, giving you full creative control.

:::tip
A healthy in-world economy has both **sinks** (ways to spend currency) and **faucets** (ways to earn it). Without balance, prices inflate and the economy feels broken. Plan your earning rates and item costs carefully.
:::
