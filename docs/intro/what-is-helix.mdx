# What is HELIX?

HELIX is a multiplayer sandbox platform that gives you the full power of a world-class game engine — without the complexity that usually comes with it. Built on **Unreal Engine 5**, HELIX handles the hard parts (networking, hosting, distribution) so you can focus on building.

Think of it this way: **HELIX turns you from a modder into a game creator.**

## Built by creators, for creators

Every tool in HELIX exists because a creator needed it. The platform was built from the ground up to remove the friction that holds modding communities back — scattered files, manual server setup, no way to get paid, and always building on someone else's game.

With HELIX, you build on your own foundation. **You own everything you create.**

## Who is it for?

- **FiveM and RageMP communities** — You already know how to build servers and write scripts. HELIX gives you a modern engine, better tools, and you actually own your work. No more building on borrowed IP.
- **Roblox creators** — You know how to build games and monetize them. HELIX gives you the visual quality and creative freedom of a professional game engine, with the same publish-and-earn model you're used to.
- **Roleplay communities** — Next-gen visuals, built-in voice, vehicles, characters, weapons, and a full economy system. Everything you need for immersive RP.
- **Game developers** — Skip the months of netcode and infrastructure work. HELIX gives you multiplayer, hosting, and distribution out of the box.
- **Content entrepreneurs** — Build worlds, publish packages, earn real money through LIX. Your creativity pays.

## What makes it different?

| | FiveM / RageMP | Roblox | HELIX |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Engine** | GTA V (2013) | Custom (limited) | Unreal Engine 5 |
| **Visuals** | Locked to GTA assets | Stylized, lower fidelity | AAA quality, film-grade rendering |
| **IP ownership** | Built on Rockstar's game | Platform owns distribution | You own everything |
| **Monetization** | Donations, Tebex | Robux (DevEx) | LIX with real cash-out |
| **Hosting** | Rent your own VPS | Handled by Roblox | One-click Instant Hosting |
| **Package sharing** | Discord links, ZIPs | Roblox toolbox | Vault (versioned, searchable) |
| **Scripting** | Lua | Luau | Blueprints, Lua, JavaScript |

## The core loop

1. **Create** — Build worlds in HELIX Studio (a customized Unreal Editor) or prototype in Build Mode
2. **Script** — Add gameplay with Blueprints, Lua, or JavaScript
3. **Publish** — Push your Packages to Vault for others to use
4. **Host** — Deploy your World with one-click Instant Hosting
5. **Earn** — Players spend LIX in your world, and you convert it to real money

No engine licensing fees. No server rental headaches. No platform lock-in on your creative work.

**Spend less time fighting limitations, and more time building what you love.**
