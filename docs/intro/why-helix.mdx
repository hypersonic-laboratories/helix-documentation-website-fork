# Why HELIX?

There are plenty of platforms out there. Here's why HELIX is different.

## A Real Game Engine, Made Accessible

HELIX runs on **Unreal Engine 5** — the same tech behind Fortnite, Rocket League, and countless AAA titles. That means film-quality graphics, realistic physics, massive open worlds, and photorealistic characters. But you don't need to be an engine programmer to use it. HELIX wraps all that power in tools designed for creators, not just engineers.

## You Own Your Content

Your assets, your scripts, your worlds — they belong to you. When you create something original in HELIX, you own the IP. No platform lock-in on your creative work. No risk of a publisher pulling the rug out from under you.

## Earn Real Money with LIX

**LIX** is the HELIX platform currency. Players spend LIX in your worlds, and you earn LIX that can be **converted to real money** through creator payouts. Your best work pays your rent. No blockchain, no crypto — just a straightforward creator economy.

## Three Ways to Script

Pick the language that fits your brain:

- **Blueprints** — Visual scripting, the primary workflow in HELIX Studio. No coding required.
- **Lua** — Lightweight and familiar. Great if you're coming from FiveM or other modding communities.
- **JavaScript** — Full JS/TS support. Great if you're a web developer.

Mix and match across your project. No wrong answers.

## Instant Hosting

Forget renting VPS boxes, configuring ports, and babysitting server processes. **Instant Hosting** gives you one-click server deployment on the HELIX network. Your world goes live in seconds — with a dedicated database, managed updates, and network-level protections included.

## Vault

A centralized **package repository and marketplace** — think npm meets the Steam Workshop. Publish, share, and sell your creations. Find assets, scripts, and full game modes from the community. No more hunting through Discord links and ZIP files.

## Built for Migration

Coming from FiveM? There's a ported **QBCore framework** ready to go. Coming from Roblox or RageMP? The scripting patterns will feel familiar. HELIX meets you where you are.
