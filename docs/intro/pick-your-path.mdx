# Pick Your Path

Everyone starts somewhere different. Find your lane and hit the ground running.

## "I'm brand new to all of this"

No worries — we've got you. Head to the **[Getting Started](/docs/getting-started/install)** guide. You'll install HELIX, create your first World, write your first script, and publish it. Step by step, no experience required.

## "I'm coming from FiveM"

Welcome aboard. You'll feel right at home. HELIX has a ported **QBCore framework**, familiar scripting patterns in Lua, and way better visuals. Check out the **[FiveM Migration Guide](/docs/migration/from-fivem)** to translate your skills.

## "I'm coming from Roblox"

You're upgrading to Unreal Engine 5 — congratulations. The scripting concepts carry over, but now you've got Nanite, Lumen, and real physics. Start with the **[Roblox Migration Guide](/docs/migration/from-roblox)** for a smooth transition.

## "I already know Unreal Engine"

Skip the basics. Dive into the **[Scripting Guide](/docs/scripting/choosing-your-language)** to learn how HELIX extends UE5 with multiplayer Packages, Vault, and multi-language scripting. You'll be productive in minutes.
