# Monetization

HELIX gives creators real ways to earn from their work through **LIX**, the platform's currency. Players buy LIX and spend it in your worlds -- and you can cash out your earnings for real money.

## What Is LIX?

LIX is the universal currency across all of HELIX. Think of it like Robux -- players purchase LIX with real money, then spend it on packages, in-world items, game passes, and more. As a creator, the LIX you earn flows directly into your creator balance.

## Setting Up Monetization

To start earning, you need to:

1. **Enable monetization** in your world settings through the HELIX Hub.
2. **Set up a creator payout account** -- link your payment details so you can withdraw earnings.
3. **Define your offerings** -- decide what you're selling and at what price.

## What Can You Sell?

There are several ways to monetize your world:

- **Vault packages** -- Sell scripts, assets, game modes, or UI kits on the [Vault](./the-vault.mdx). Set a LIX price and earn from every purchase.
- **In-world purchases** -- Sell items, abilities, cosmetics, or access passes directly inside your world. Players can buy things through in-game UI you build.
- **Game passes** -- Offer one-time purchases that unlock premium features or VIP access.
- **Subscriptions** -- Set up recurring LIX charges for ongoing perks.

## Pricing Your Content

When setting prices, keep in mind:

- **Know your audience.** Casual worlds with younger players do better with lower price points and high volume.
- **Offer value.** Players are happy to spend when they feel they're getting something meaningful.
- **Use tiered pricing.** Give players options -- a basic tier and a premium tier work well together.
- **Don't lock core gameplay behind paywalls.** Players leave worlds that feel pay-to-win. Monetize cosmetics, convenience, and extras instead.

## Creator Payouts

The LIX you earn can be converted to real money. Here's the flow:

1. Players spend LIX in your world or on your Vault packages.
2. Your creator balance increases.
3. Once you hit the minimum payout threshold, you can request a withdrawal.
4. Funds are transferred to your linked payment account.

HELIX takes a platform fee on transactions (displayed transparently in the Hub), and the rest is yours. Payout schedules and minimums are detailed in your creator dashboard.

## Best Practices

- **Be transparent.** Show players what they're buying before they spend LIX.
- **Test your pricing.** Start with moderate prices and adjust based on conversion data in your analytics.
- **Reward engagement.** Give free players a taste of premium content so they're motivated to buy.
- **Keep it fair.** Worlds that balance free and paid content build bigger, more loyal communities.
